Color

The palette is small and every color is muted. The accent is the only colorful thing on a site; everything else is a cool gray. Use the dots in the toolbar to change the accent of the whole reference site.

Accents

Eight hues at OKLCH L 0.76 / C 0.06 on dark and L 0.52 / C 0.07 on light. Equal lightness means buttons and links have the same contrast on every site (about 9:1 on dark and 5:1 on light). Graphite is a near-neutral extra. The ratios are against the page background.

Each site picks one accent; everything else is shared. An accent marks a family of related sites (dev tools, games, writing), so sites in one family may share it; names, icons and the mascots tell them apart.

Near-neutral accents

When the hues run out, add near-neutrals: the same lightness (so the same contrast) at C 0.02 (0.025 on light), nearly gray. Keep them at least 70° apart or they become hard to tell apart.

Chart colors

Five series colors, for data marks only (bars, fills, legend keys), used in a fixed order. Neighbors differ in lightness as well as hue, so they stay apart for color-blind readers; slot 1 is the brightest on dark and the deepest on light. This is the one place facet goes above C 0.08: series colors sit at C 0.105, just past the floor (0.10) below which a hue reads as gray. Text never wears a series color.

1 · Slate 石板 --f-chart-1 · hue 255 · L 0.665 / 0.44
2 · Heather 石南 --f-chart-2 · hue 330 · L 0.54 / 0.62
3 · Clay 陶土 --f-chart-3 · hue 55 · L 0.6 / 0.53
4 · Lavender 薰衣草 --f-chart-4 · hue 295 · L 0.665 / 0.44
5 · Sage 鼠尾草 --f-chart-5 · hue 165 · L 0.54 / 0.62

Checked with a palette validator against --f-bg-2 in both themes: neighbors stay ΔE ≥ 13 apart under simulated protanopia and deuteranopia and ΔE ≥ 15.5 for full color vision, all at 3:1 contrast or more; the first three also pass pairwise, for charts where any two marks can touch. The ratios are against --f-bg-2.

Magnitude ramp

Magnitude (a heatmap, ordered buckets) takes one hue in five lightness steps, faint to strong: --f-seq-1 to --f-seq-5. Hue and chroma come from the accent, so the ramp follows the site; try the dots in the toolbar. The faintest step still keeps 2:1 against the background.
